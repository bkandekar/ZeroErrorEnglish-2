SSC CGL Tier-II Examiner Trap in Action

Neither of the two candidates have submitted has submitted their application on time.

Governing Rule

"Neither of" denotes one of two and strictly governs a singular verb (has) and a singular pronoun, regardless of the plural noun phrase following it.

Pedagogical Foundation

Why Rote Memorization Fails in Competitive English

Most aspirants study grammar by memorizing abstract rules in isolation. In high-pressure examinations like SSC CGL and Banking PO, examiners never test simple rules; they test distractor traps designed to fool your intuitive ear.

I

Contrastive Error Analysis

We never show a correct rule without immediately presenting the incorrect counterfeit examiners use to disguise it. By training your eye to recognize the distractor, you eliminate false positives.

II

Structural Parsing Over Intuition

When a sentence stretches to 35 words with four prepositional phrases, your ear will associate the verb with the nearest noun rather than the true subject. We teach mechanical syntactic bracket-stripping.

III

Single-Issue Topic Handbooks

Instead of a monolithic 600-page textbook where rules get lost, the 14 Kindle handbooks divide the English language into modular, drillable domains you can master one evening at a time.
