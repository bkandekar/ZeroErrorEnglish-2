Examiner's Trap #1

Examiners deliberately insert intervening prepositional phrases to make singular subjects look plural. Learn the exact elimination technique in Chapter 2 of this guide.

✕ Common Error Found in Mock Tests
The list of candidate names were published yesterday.
"Were" is grammatically flawed. The subject is "list" (singular).
✓ Exam-Accurate Version
The list of candidate names was published yesterday.
Correct agreement between "list" and "was".
